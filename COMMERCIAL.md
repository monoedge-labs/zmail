# Commercial use

[简体中文](COMMERCIAL.zh-CN.md)

Zmail is free for noncommercial use under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE): personal use, study, research,
hobby projects, and use by charities, schools and public bodies.

Using Zmail for a commercial purpose, such as inside a company's business
operations, in a product or service you sell, or as part of paid work, needs a
separate commercial license from the maintainer.

To ask for one, open an issue in
[monoedge-labs/zmail](https://github.com/monoedge-labs/zmail/issues) titled
"Commercial license". Say who will use it and for what. Do not put private
details in the issue; the maintainer will reply there with how to continue.
